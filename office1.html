Before you buy: a five-point check

  1. Match the product to your device. Check operating-system version, available storage, and whether the listing is for Windows, macOS, or another platform.
  2. Read the license scope. Confirm the number of users and devices, term, region, language, and whether the product is a subscription or perpetual license.
  3. Check the included apps. Do not assume every product includes the same desktop apps, cloud storage, mobile use, or premium features.
  4. Understand delivery and activation. A digital purchase should clearly state how and when you receive a license key or redemption instructions. Never share your key publicly.
  5. Review the return terms first. Digital software may have activation-related return limits. Read our Refund Policy and Terms & Conditions before ordering.

Safe installation basics

Only download installers from the publisher’s official download or account portal. Sign in with the account you intend to keep, redeem the license exactly as instructed, and retain your receipt and confirmation email.

Be cautious of unsolicited calls, remote-access requests, or pop-ups claiming that your software needs urgent repair. A legitimate retailer will not ask for your account password or payment details over an unexpected support call.

If activation does not work, stop before purchasing another key. Check the product instructions, confirm the account and region requirements, and contact the seller through the published contact page with your order details.

Questions to ask any software retailer

What exactly am I receiving?

Look for a specific product name, license term, device count, delivery method, price, and any restrictions before checkout.

Who is the seller?

Verify the business name, contact details, privacy information, and refund process. A clear independent-seller disclosure helps prevent confusion with the publisher.

Where can I get product support?

Ask whether support is for the order and delivery process or for the software itself. Publisher account and technical issues may need official publisher support.
